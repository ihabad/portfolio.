Unusual Funds Movement Alert Review

Scenario

A fictional retail customer with historically modest payroll activity suddenly receives multiple peer-to-peer transfers and cash deposits, followed by a large outbound international wire.

Review Objective

Determine whether the activity is consistent with the customer’s expected profile, identify material red flags, document the observed pattern, and decide whether the training alert should be closed or escalated.

Key Observations
  • 17 incoming P2P transfers from unrelated parties within 30 days.
  • Cash deposits materially above the customer’s historical activity.
  • Rapid concentration of incoming funds before an outbound international wire.
  • Activity inconsistent with the fictional customer’s expected salaried profile.
Case Conclusion

Escalate for additional review. The combined pattern is not explained by the fictional customer profile and warrants deeper source-of-funds and counterparty analysis.

Cash Activity & Structuring Pattern Review

Scenario

A fictional small-business account shows repeated cash deposits over a short period, followed by consolidation and outbound transfers.

Review Objective

Review the activity as a pattern rather than relying on any single transaction amount, compare it with expected business activity, and document whether the behavior warrants escalation.

Key Observations
  • Multiple cash deposits occur across a compressed time period.
  • Deposit values are similar and collectively material to the account.
  • Funds are consolidated and moved out shortly after deposit activity.
  • The fictional business profile does not fully explain the frequency and timing.
Case Conclusion

Escalate the pattern for enhanced review. The decision is based on frequency, timing, customer context, and subsequent movement of funds | not one isolated amount.

Rapid Movement / Pass-Through Account Review

Scenario

A fictional account receives funds from multiple unrelated parties and sends most of the value outward within one business day, leaving a low ending balance.

Review Objective

Assess whether the account is being used primarily as a pass-through vehicle, identify key counterparties, measure rapid movement, and document the rationale for escalation or closure.

Key Observations
  • Incoming credits originate from several unrelated counterparties.
  • A large percentage of incoming value exits within hours.
  • The account repeatedly returns to a low residual balance.
  • The fictional customer profile does not indicate a payment-processing or money-services purpose.
Case Conclusion

Escalate for deeper review of source and purpose of funds and relationships among counterparties.

P2P / Money-Mule Pattern Review

Scenario

A fictional personal account begins receiving repeated P2P payments from numerous unrelated senders, then transfers most funds to a small number of external destinations.

Review Objective

Identify sender concentration, transaction frequency, consolidation behavior, and whether the activity fits the customer’s stated purpose and prior history.

Key Observations
  • High number of unrelated P2P senders compared with historical activity.
  • Incoming payments cluster over several days.
  • Funds are consolidated and sent to a small set of beneficiaries.
  • Little evidence of ordinary personal spending between inflows and outflows.
Case Conclusion

Escalate for further review because the activity resembles a collection-and-transfer pattern inconsistent with the fictional personal account profile.
